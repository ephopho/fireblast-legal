Fire Blast: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Fire Blast is a target-shooting game that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone or iPad so it can remember your progress:

  • Your best championship score.
  • A championship you have started but not finished, so you can resume it later.
  • The latest copy of the game's settings file (see Network below).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

Fire Blast works fully offline. When a connection is available it downloads one small, public settings file (game balance values and an optional news line) from GitHub Pages (ephopho.github.io) when it starts, and at most every 30 minutes after that. The request sends no personal information, identifiers, or game data, and we receive nothing from it. As with any web request, GitHub, which hosts the file, sees your IP address and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game makes no other network requests, and the game engine's analytics, crash reporting, and performance reporting services are turned off.

Purchases

Fire Blast has no in-app purchases.

Children

Fire Blast does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
