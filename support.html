How to shoot

  • Aim: drag anywhere on the screen. The crosshair stays in the middle; the target moves under it.
  • Steady: tap STEADY to hold your breath. The sights come up, the wobble settles, and the breath bar drains. Fire before it runs out.
  • Fire: the trigger goes the moment you touch FIRE.
  • Lying and kneeling wobble less than standing. Pistols wobble the most.

Common questions

  • How is a practice scored? Each practice shows its scoring rings or rectangles on the target and its highest possible score (HPS). A white spotting disc marks your latest shot.
  • How does the championship work? You shoot all four matches, every practice in each, for your chosen service against two AI services. Each match counts out of 100 and the best total out of 400 wins.
  • I left in the middle of a championship. Open the game and tap RESUME CHAMPIONSHIP on the title screen. A practice you were part way through starts again; finished practices are kept.
  • How do I pause? Tap the pause button at the top left. The game also pauses itself if you leave the app.
